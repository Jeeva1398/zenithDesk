🛡 Security

Built so one team never sees another's data

Every record belongs to one organization, and every query is scoped to it by design - with a guard that refuses any query that forgets. Sessions are short-lived and rotate, and the public-facing parts are rate limited.

  • Org-scoped queries
  • Rotating sessions
  • One-time email codes
  • Rate limiting
  • HTTPS everywhere

Questions, answered

Does the bot make up answers? +

No. It only answers from the knowledge-base articles you publish, and it has to quote the sentence it relied on. When your articles don't cover a question, it says so and offers a ticket or an enquiry instead.

Can I use it only for sales enquiries? +

Yes. In Settings → Chatbot, choose "Enquiry only". The bot takes down name, email or phone, and company, and anything else gets a polite reply in your own words - for example, where to go for support.

How do I add the chat widget to my website? +

Copy the one-line snippet from Settings → Chat widget and paste it before the closing </body> tag on your site, then list that site under Allowed sites.

Can customers see their tickets? +

Yes - through the customer portal, or by asking the chat widget. Either way they confirm their email with a one-time code first.

Who gets told about a new enquiry? +

Only the email address you set as your enquiry alert address. Every enquiry is also listed on the Enquiries page, where your team can mark it contacted or closed and keep notes.
